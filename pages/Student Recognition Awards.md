<p><strong>Recognising Good Character</strong>
<br>Do you know a classmate who always does the right thing, even when no
one is watching? Someone who lifts others up, stands firm in their values,
or goes out of their way to contribute to the community around them?
<br>
</p>
<p>The <strong>Edusave Character Award (ECHA)</strong> recognises students
who demonstrate exemplary character through their actions and behaviour,
not their grades or achievements. We are looking for students who embody
good values, show civic-mindedness, or demonstrate resilience in the face
of challenges.
<br>
</p>
<p>If someone comes to mind, we would love to hear from you. Submit your
nomination using the attached link. Tell us what you have observed and
why you think this classmate deserves to be recognised.

</p>
<p>Nominations close <strong>31 July 2026, 2359h</strong>.</p>
